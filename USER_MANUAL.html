OFFICIAL OPERATIONAL GUIDE • FACULTY, HOD & PRINCIPAL DESK

1. Faculty & Class Teachers Operational Guide

Step 1.1: Self-Registration & Coordinator Profile

If you are a new faculty member and do not yet have login credentials:

  1. Open portal homepage: https://ssits-edupulse.onrender.com
  2. Tap MENU (☰) on top-right → Select Faculty Self-Registration.
  3. Enter your Full Name, Mobile Number, Email, Assigned Program (Diploma / B.Tech), Department, Academic Year, and Section.
  4. Choose your username and password, then click Submit Registration Request.
  5. Once Administrator approves, your account is immediately active.
Step 1.2: Sign In & Google Authenticator (2FA Setup)

To protect college records, Two-Factor Authentication is mandatory:

  1. Open MENU (☰) → Faculty Sign In. Enter your username and password.
  2. First Login: Install Google Authenticator app on your smartphone from Play Store / App Store.
  3. Tap the + button in Google Authenticator, choose Scan a QR code, and scan the QR code on your computer screen.
  4. Enter the 6-digit code showing on your phone to link your account.
  5. On future logins, simply enter the 6-digit code generated on your phone.
Step 1.3: Daily Attendance Marking (FN & AN Sessions)
  1. Select the session: Morning Session (FN) or Afternoon Session (AN).
  2. By default, all enrolled students in your section are pre-marked PRESENT (Green).
  3. Simply tap on any student who is absent to toggle their status to ABSENT (Red).
  4. Click Save Attendance. Present percentage and absentees count update instantly.
Step 1.4: 1-Click WhatsApp Absentee Alerts to Parents

Notify parents immediately after attendance is submitted:

  1. Click the Send Parent WhatsApp Alerts button.
  2. Review the auto-generated bilingual absentee notification for the student.
  3. Click Send Alert to dispatch the official notification directly to the parent's WhatsApp.
Step 1.5: Declaring Holidays & Special College Events

If college is closed due to a festival or Sunday, use Day Status & Academic Occasion to mark "Holiday" and give the occasion name (e.g., Pongal / Independence Day). This ensures holiday dates are not counted as working days.

Step 1.6: Generating Official Monthly Reports & Dossiers

Open the Reports tab, choose the Month & Year, and click Download Cumulative Monthly PDF for official JNTUA/SBTET compliant attendance sheets with full daily grid and percentage calculations.

2. Head of Department (HOD) Operational Guide

Step 2.1: HOD Access & Department Selection
  1. Tap MENU (☰) → HOD Portal (or visit /hod/login).
  2. Select your Department (e.g., ECE, CSE, EEE, MECH, CIVIL, AI&DS).
  3. Enter your HOD username, password, and Google Authenticator 2FA code.
  4. Your dashboard immediately greets you with: Head of Department: [Your Name].
Step 2.2: Dual Academic Oversight (Diploma + B.Tech)

HODs have full unified visibility over all 3 years of Diploma and all 4 years of B.Tech. View live class-by-class present percentages, total strength, and coordinator status in real time.

Step 2.3: Department Daily Absentees Monitoring

Select any date to see the consolidated list of all absentees across all years in your department. View which class teacher marked them and access parent phone numbers for follow-ups.

3. Principal & Director Executive Desk Guide

Step 3.1: Executive Desk Login
  1. Tap MENU (☰) → Principal Portal (or visit /principal/login).
  2. Enter Principal credentials and 6-digit Google Authenticator code.
  3. Dashboard displays: Principal: Dr. Principal / Director (SSITS).
Step 3.2: Institution-Wide College Governance

Provides top-level counters: Total Enrolled Strength across all branches, Today's Overall College Attendance Percentage, and a live Branch Comparison Matrix comparing attendance across ECE, CSE, EEE, MECH, CIVIL, and AIDS.
